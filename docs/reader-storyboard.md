# One-token reader: per-stop storyboard

## Header

**Purpose.** For each of the 43 stops, one row stating what the viewer is trying to learn, what the camera frames, what lights up (mechanism, pin label, color role) and what the narration says. Section 3 lists where those four layers disagree.

**Source commit.** 4313930 (branch `feature/spatial-incremental`). Line references are to that commit. This document describes the reader as built and changes no code or narration.

**Sources.** `one-token/stops-manifest.js` (stops, `spatialBeat`), `one-token/scene-spatial.js` (`B` table at 654, `STEP_BEATS` 738, `TAGS` 747, `SUB_BEATS` 846, `locate()` 854, camera presets 1047-1130, pin selection 1146-1154), `one-token/narration-data.js` (clips and routes), `index.html` (`data-step` articles), `shared/theme-tokens.js` and `DESIGN.md` (color roles).

**How to read the table.**
- *Viewer question* is editorial, inferred from the written step title and text. It is not a repo field.
- *Camera* gives the beat key. `A→B` means a SUB_BEATS split: on scroll (not narrated) the first beat covers localT 0-0.55 and the second 0.55-1. Narrated playback uses the route's own beat and never splits (`scene-spatial.js:858`). Poses are `eye→target`. Unless noted the **director** preset applies: it keeps the authored view direction, re-centres on the token, the pin anchors, `fit` boxes, and the tower only when the beat has `tower` and `frameTower !== false` (`interestPoints`, 884-888; `directorFit`, 1047). Wide `A` beats and the follow beat `3f` skip the fit. `loopJ`/`parkJ`/`scrubJ` set how the token's journey position moves: loop = scrub along the segment with stop progress, park = hold, scrub = full-range follow.
- *Lights up* gives the `show` mechanisms and the pin. The pin is `tags[min(n-1, floor(u*n))]` (line 1147), so multi-tag beats swap their label as the reader scrolls through the stop. A beat with no tags falls back to `“ Paris”` (token visible) or `model weights` (1152-1153). Color role: Vermilion = token, Cobalt = attention, Brass = KV cache / serving, Pine = tool results, Terracotta = gradients / mask / fault / training phase.
- *Narration* is the one-line gist of the full route (`narration-data.js` routes.full); "quick" notes where the Swiss route differs. Every stop has a cue in both routes, but several cues are best-fit cuts inside a longer cue (flagged in section 3).
- A color-role finding that needs a visual check is marked **unverified**. Role assignments here come from material references in the source, not from rendered frames.

**Beat-reuse summary (manifest `spatialBeat`).** 30 distinct beat keys serve 43 stops. 11 keys are shared by 24 stops: nine keys shared by two stops (1b, 1c, 2a, 3c, 3e, 4b, 6a, 6c, 6d) and two shared by three (6b, 6e). 19 stops own a beat alone. `STEP_BEATS` gives 16 of the 24 shared stops a variant beat that changes the pin and `show`, and sometimes the camera. The other 8 shared stops (1.2, 2.1, 3.1, 5.1, 8.1, 9.1, 10.1, 10.2) use the base beat. The base beats `3c`, `4b` and `6e` are never selected on scroll because every stop that points to them is remapped.

## 2. Cross-cutting camera notes

- **Preset branches** (`scene-spatial.js:1044-1073`). `director` re-centres on the token, pin anchors, `fit` boxes and (when the beat has `tower`) the tower. `overhead` keeps the authored target and flattens the eye to `y = 0.88 × distance`. `close` (and phone `compact`) only scales the authored offset by 0.69 (0.79 compact). `fit` boxes, `ATTENTION_PLATE`, `EXPERT_PLATE` and `POST_STATION` therefore take effect only under `director`. Whether close or overhead still frame the mechanism in 7.2, 7.3, 8.2, 10.2 and 12.1 is **unverified** from static reading.
- **Pin cycling.** Multi-tag beats change their pin by progress, not by narration. 1.1 cycles five labels; 7.2 cycles three in its second half.
- **Fallback pin.** Only 5.3 (`3f`) has `tags: []` among the 43 stops.
